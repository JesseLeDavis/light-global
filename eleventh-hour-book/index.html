The parable of the laborers

About the Book

A divine moment.
An open invitation.

We find ourselves in a divine moment in human history, where God is awakening a generation to their true purpose, calling, and destiny. In one of His parables, Jesus spoke of these as the "eleventh hour" laborers He is recruiting to join Him in His great work of drawing people of all nations to Himself.

This is not merely a call to work — it is a call to relationship, partnership, and transformation.

"There is no more fulfilling experience than coming home to a loving Father and journeying in close personal relationship with Him."

All of creation is longing to see transformed people who are transforming culture, and that is God's purpose in this generation. It's time to hear the call and embark together on a journey to see the Father's kingdom come and His will be done.

From the Introduction

"Some of the particulars of my life story may be unique. The invitation behind it is not."

As I have discovered for myself in answering this call, there is no more fulfilling experience than coming home to a loving Father and journeying in a close personal relationship with Him and those He calls friends.

While some of the particulars of my life story may be unique, it is also another example of how God invites all of us — including you — to be part of a much greater story He is writing.
